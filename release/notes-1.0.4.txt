Greycell Achievements 1.0.4
- New default unlock sound, Echo, plus marimba and retro 8-bit versions.
- Rare achievements: fewer than 1% of players (or 5%, 10%) get a silver popup with how rare they are, and their own sound.
- Popups show the achievement's own picture.
- PlayStation trophies no longer pop up on the PC (they are earned on the console).
